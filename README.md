## 1. **The Amazing Spider-Man (2014)**

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Superhero, Sci‑Fi

**Rating:** PG‑13 (Parents Strongly Cautioned)

**Description:**

The Amazing Spider-Man 2 follows Peter Parker as he balances life as Spider-Man and his relationship with Gwen Stacy. When a dangerous new villain called Electro threatens New York City, Peter must protect the people he loves while uncovering secrets about his parents' past. Along the way, he also faces his childhood friend Harry Osborn, whose transformation leads to unexpected consequences.

**My Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐☆☆ (8/10)

**Short Fact:**

This movie features the final appearance of Andrew Garfield as Spider-Man in his solo film series before the character was rebooted in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

## 2. **Avengers: Endgame (2019)**

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Superhero, Sci‑Fi

**Rating:** PG‑13 (Parents Strongly Cautioned)

**Description:**

Avengers: Endgame follows the surviving Avengers after the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War. With half of all life erased by Thanos, the heroes must reunite and embark on a daring mission to restore the universe. Along the way, they face difficult sacrifices, revisit moments from the past, and prepare for an epic final battle that will determine the fate of everyone.

**My Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (10/10)

**Short Fact:**

Avengers: Endgame became one of the highest-grossing films of all time, earning about $2.94 billion worldwide.

## 3. **Solo Leveling (2024)**

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Urban Fantasy

**Rating:** R-17+ (Violence & Profanity)

**Description:**

Solo Leveling follows Sung Jin-Woo, the weakest E-rank hunter in a world filled with monsters and mysterious gates. After surviving a dangerous dungeon, he gains access to a unique system that allows him to grow stronger without limits. As he levels up, Jin-Woo transforms from the weakest hunter into one of humanity's most powerful defenders.

**My Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (10/10)

**Short Fact:**

Solo Leveling is based on a South Korean web novel written by Chugong and was later adapted into a highly popular webtoon and anime series.
